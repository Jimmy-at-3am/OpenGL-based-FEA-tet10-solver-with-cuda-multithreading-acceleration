PolyFEA overview

PolyFEA predicts how a 3D-printed part bends and where it breaks. It hands the part to Bambu Studio for slicing, then simulates the part as it is printed: its walls, its infill and its layers. The bond between layers is weaker than the plastic itself, so PolyFEA models that bond as a separate, weaker link.
