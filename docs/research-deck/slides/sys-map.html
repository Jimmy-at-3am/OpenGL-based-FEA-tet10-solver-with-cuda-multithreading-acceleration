The front end sits in the middle and drives every module through the Inspector tabs (grey dashed). Blue arrows are the data flow from file to result. Magenta arrows show what the Bambu Studio bridge hands out from one sliced package: G-code toolpaths to the toolpath module, the plain model and PartPlacement frames to picking and load transfer, and the print summary with its material card to the solver.
