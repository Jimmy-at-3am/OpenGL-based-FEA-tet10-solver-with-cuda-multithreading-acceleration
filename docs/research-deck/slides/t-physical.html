Status: proposed, not yet run · coupons calibrate the material first, predictions are committed before anything else breaks

From the physical validation test plan (milestone 1, reviewed 4 October 2026). Equipment: education-kit masses (5 g steps), exercise weights (5 lb steps), an all-PLA wedge lowerer that lowers the plates 1 mm per 8 mm of pull so hands never carry the load, nylon cord, a bench vise and an overhead bar. About 45 test pieces plus 10 fixture and loader parts, all printed with one profile: 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 2 walls, 100 % rectilinear infill. Expected results written before testing: stiffness within ±10 % once E, E_z and G_pz come from these prints; flat bar and C-ring breaking loads likely under-predicted (measured 1.2–2× predicted) because printed PLA is stronger in bending than in tension; columns carry 30–80× less than the solver predicts because it has no buckling.
