Auto-switch a surface vertex is curved past 15°:

Tet4 (TetrahedralElement) puts the four node positions into P; C = P inverse gives the constant shape-function slopes. Tet10 (Tet10Element) uses barycentric shape functions and the 4-point Hammer rule with points built from a and b. solveAdaptive switches the whole mesh to Tet10 when more than 25 % of surface vertices are curved (curvatureAngleThreshold = 15°, highCurvatureFracLimit = 0.25).
