Picking loads on the clean CAD model

groupIntoCadFaces(mesh)

Job split the clean CAD triangles into the faces a person expects, by fixed rules: same result every time

1 weld vertices that share a position

2 a sharp edge bends more than 30° (30.01° with rounding slack) and always separates faces

3 flat face: corners lie within 10⁻⁴ × part diagonal of one plane; area-weighted normals within 1°

4 round face (hole, shaft, fillet): normals at right angles to one axis within 1°, corners equally far from it; at least 5 distinct corners, since 3 or 4 points always fit a circle

5 designed flats inside a round region (a D-shaft's flat) become separate flat faces

6 anything else becomes planar pieces or one curved face; regions are grouped with union-find [21]

Result box → 6 faces · round hole → 1 face · sphere → 1 face

n face normal · xc corner · D part diagonal · a round-face axis · r its radius

What each picking tool runs

Point

pickUnderCursor(ray) → nearest point on the hit triangle [12]; it turns yellow under the mouse

Face

pickUnderCursor(ray) → faceOfTriangle(t): the whole CAD face lights up; Shift+click adds or removes faces

Create plane

placePlaneAt(hit): on a flat face the plane lies on the face; on a round or curved face it touches at the clicked point (nearestFaceTo(point, normal))

Move in ℝ³

plane handle: 3 arrows slide along X, Y, Z; 3 rings turn it; Ctrl snaps 1 mm and 15°

Pieces

intersectPlane(plane): line (cuts a face) → force per length · area (lies on a face) → force per area · touch line (round face) → line load · touch point (curved face) → point load

Draw plane geometry builds the plane square, touch piece and hovered face; they render translucent yellow and blue

Load Setup panel “+” adds a condition; Choose where it acts arms picking; typing a number keeps hover alive; undo / redo; the receipt redraws every frame; a new mesh rebuilds its data on the worker thread

Older picker worked on the mesh surface (flat face, hole wall, single facet); still compiled and unit-tested

Real names: PlainModel with build(mesh), pick(ray), faceOf(triangle), nearestFace(point, normal), placePlane(hit), meet(plane); PlaneHandle (kMoveSnapMM = 1 mm, kTurnSnapDeg = 15°), PlaneGeometry, PlaneRender; LoadSetupUI with buildMeshState(); LoadSetupSelection is the older picker. Picking always happens on the plain CAD model; the next slide moves each pick onto the printed mesh.
